03 / SOLUTION

Introducing AquaSense

An end-to-end Earth Observation pipeline that ingests Sentinel-2, computes NDWI, and delivers hydrological change with AI ecological synthesis.

🛰️STAC Auto-Discovery — Queries Planetary Computer for best low-cloud scenes with retry-on-failure
🌊NDWI Intelligence — (B03-B08)/(B03+B08) with dynamic thresholding & 5 color ramps
📊Pixel Quantification — Real-time km², % change, longitudinal trend 2019-2025
🧠Gemini Ecological AI — 4 modes: Deep Reasoning, Search, Maps, Fast Summary
🗺️Interactive Observatory — Drag BBOX, split-slider, diff mask (gain/loss)
📸Field Verification — Multimodal understanding for ground-truthing
